Boilerplate

50 words

Redirect is an Android app that helps people quit porn, one urge at a time. It can block known adult sites on the phone, and when an urge shows up it offers a 90-second guided pause, then a plan the person wrote on a calmer day. No account. No streaks. No ads.

200 words

Redirect is a private Android app for adults who want to quit porn on their own terms. When an urge shows up, one tap from the app, a Quick Settings tile, or a widget starts a 90-second pause: breathe with a slow ring alongside Pip, the app's caterpillar, name what's underneath, then pick an if-then plan written on a calmer day.

There is no account, and no reports go to anyone. Journals, check-ins, and plans stay on the phone unless the person exports or shares them, and can be deleted at any time. There are no streaks: a hard night goes in the log as one night, and nothing resets.

Two optional guardrails add friction the person chooses: site blocking, which blocks known adult sites on the phone, and app pauses, which put a pause card over chosen apps. A waiting period can delay changes that lower protection. It has limits: it isn't treatment or parental control, and it isn't tamper-proof.

Redirect is free, and made in South Africa.
