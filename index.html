Blocking is the easy part. The urge is the hard part.

A blocker can stop a page from loading. It can't do much about the pull that's still there afterwards, phone in hand. That moment is what Redirect is built for.

So when a site is blocked, Redirect can offer a pause: a quiet notification that never names the site, or the pause card if app pauses are on. You can turn the notification off.

Ninety seconds, four small steps.

Start a pause from the app, a Quick Settings tile or a home-screen widget. Each step is skippable, and there is nothing to decide until you're ready.

  1. 1Breathe

    In for four, out for six, with gentle vibrations if you like them. Pip breathes with you.

  2. 2Name it

    Tired, lonely, stressed, bored. It helps you spot patterns later, and "Not sure" is always an answer.

  3. 3Your plan

    Pick a plan you wrote on a calmer day. The ones that fit what you named come first.

  4. 4Record

    Note how it ended, as information, never as a verdict. A hard answer is as useful as an easy one.

Plans written on a calmer day.

If-then plans are one of the better-studied ways to change a habit. You write them when you're calm. When an urge arrives, they're the first thing you see.

Why Redirect has no streaks

Two guardrails, and where each one stops.

Redirect works without any special access. Both guardrails are off until you turn them on.

What it doesWhere it stops

Site blocking

What it does

An on-device VPN checks your phone's lookups (the site names it asks for) against the community-maintained OISD list of known adult sites. A site on your Allowed sites list is let through. Android shows a VPN indicator while it is on. When a site is blocked, a quiet notification can offer a pause.

Where it stops

It waits while Private DNS is set to a named provider. Custom resolvers, IPv6 DNS, DNS-over-TCP, and some apps can bypass it. Redirect does not inspect images, video, or page content.

App pauses

What it does

For apps you pick, Redirect notices when one opens and puts your pause card in front of it.

Where it stops

The pause can be passed through and the service can be disabled. It does not read screen text, take screenshots, type, or perform gestures.

You can set a waiting period ahead of time, so a change made in a hard moment takes a while to take effect. How blocking works

What you write stays on your phone.

There's no Redirect account, no analytics, no ads and no cloud sync. Your journal, check-ins, urge history and settings are stored only on your phone.

Stored locally

App data stays in private storage, with screenshots, Recents previews, and Android app-data backups restricted.

Yours to export

Save a copy of your records as a file, wherever you choose. The exported file then lives outside Redirect.

Yours to erase

Delete journals, check-ins, urge events, profile details, Allowed sites, and Protection settings from inside the app.

The fine print: lookups that aren't blocked are handed to Android's own resolver, so your Android DNS settings apply, and a blocklist update you request contacts the OISD host over HTTPS. Lookups and exported files are explained in full in the privacy policy.

What Redirect can and can't do.

Redirect can

  • Start a pause from the app, a Quick Settings tile, or a home-screen widget: breathe, name what's underneath, and choose one thing to do for the next ten minutes.
  • Put the if-then plans you wrote on a calmer day first when an urge arrives.
  • Block known adult sites with site blocking: an optional on-device VPN that uses the OISD list. Android shows a VPN indicator while it is on.
  • Put your pause card over apps you choose (app pauses), using Android Accessibility to see only which app is open.
  • Keep check-ins, your journal, and your patterns on your phone, with no account, and let you export or delete all of it.

Redirect can't

  • Block everything. Secure DNS in some browsers, other VPNs, and some apps can bypass site blocking.
  • Look at images or video.
  • Stop you turning it off for good. It is not tamper-proof software: every guardrail is off until you turn it on. On the default level it turns off straight away; a stricter level waits for the delay you chose, and uninstalling always removes it.
  • Replace medical treatment or crisis support, or act as parental control. It is a self-support tool for the adult using it.
