Breathe first. Then three short steps.

Start a pause from Home, a Quick Settings tile or a home-screen widget. It opens on the breathing screen at the top of this page: in for four, out for six, with Pip. Every step after that can be skipped.

  1. Start a pause

    One tap from Home. Nothing to decide yet.

  2. Name it

    Tired, lonely, bored, or “Not sure”. It shows you patterns later.

  3. Your plan

    The plans that fit what you named come first.

  4. Record

    How did it end? A hard answer is as useful as an easy one.

Plans written on a calmer day.

Write down what you'll do the next time a familiar moment comes round. In a pause, Your plan puts the ones that fit what you named first.

Suggestions come from your own log. The plans list shows which ones you used and how those moments ended.

Nothing resets.

There is no streak and no score. A hard night goes in your log as one night, and everything before it is still there.

See when urges show up, what you named most and how those moments ended, over 7, 30 or 90 days. Quick check-ins and a private journal sit alongside.

Why Redirect has no streaks

Two guardrails, and where each one stops.

The pause needs no special access. The guardrails are optional, and both are off until you turn them on.

What it doesWhere it stops

Site blocking

What it does

An on-device VPN checks your phone's lookups (the site names it asks for) against the OISD list of known adult sites. Allowed sites get through. Android shows a VPN indicator while it is on, and a quiet notification that never names the site can offer a pause.

Where it stops

It can't run while Private DNS is set to a named provider. Secure DNS in a browser, another VPN, IPv6 DNS and some apps get around it. It never looks at images, video or page content.

App pauses

What it does

For apps you pick, Redirect notices when one opens and puts your pause card in front of it.

Where it stops

It sees only which app is open. It doesn't read screen text, take screenshots, type or tap.

Turning them off

What it does

On Gentle, the default, a change takes effect straight away and “Open anyway” works after 10 seconds. On Committed, a change that lowers protection waits for the delay you chose, and “Open anyway” isn't offered.

Where it stops

Uninstalling always removes it, and App pauses can be switched off in Android Settings. Redirect isn't tamper-proof, and it isn't parental control.
